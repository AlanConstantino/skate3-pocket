An unofficial Android fork of Andrew Nakas's Skate 3 Android, based on his v0.1.19 release.

Andrew Nakas — the Android application, platform work, and the engine release used here.

Alex McHugh / Skate3Recomp — the Skate 3 recompilation and native renderer. Also thanks to Alex's Skate-specific runtime and Andrew's runtime fork.

ReXGlue and Xenia — the recompilation tools, runtime foundations, and Xbox 360 research.

Mesa / Turnip and MrPurple — the Vulkan drivers, including bundled T30. Billy Laws / libadrenotools — custom Adreno driver loading and linker namespace support. SDL — input, audio, and windowing.

Alan Constantino — this fork's driver manager, handheld integration, maintenance, and testing.

Skate 3 is a game by EA Black Box / Electronic Arts. You must supply your own game files.
